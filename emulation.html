» Emulators, side by side

the 10 picks, record against record

The guides above argue in prose; this is the fight card. Pick any two of the cabinet's 11 emulator picks and their real records sit side by side, systems, platforms, license, the BIOS story, each project's own notes. The verdict strip reports only what the records support: a bigger listed footprint, a BIOS one side needs and the other doesn't, a license difference. No scores, no rankings, no winner. Where a record describes its reach in words ("Dozens via cores"), the words are shown verbatim and left uncounted, a shrug is never compared against a number. Games get the same treatment at Versus.

The deal is seeded, not random: an FNV-1a hash of your seed plus the sorted emulator ids feeds a mulberry32 generator, so the same seed against the same catalog deals the same pairing every time. The seed rides in the pairing link and on the share card, re-deal it and check our work.

Starter pairing on the marquee: the cabinet's first two picks. Change either corner, or deal a seed, and it is your card.

Share card

Plain text, ready to paste into the group chat. It carries only the rows and verdict lines above.
