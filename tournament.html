Interactive tool

Game night, settled.

Single- or double-elimination brackets for 2–16 players. Seed manually, pick a game per round (or let players vote), set best-of-N, tap to advance winners. Saves in your browser — print it, copy it, or share a link to the live bracket.

Everything runs in your browser — no accounts, no uploads. Game options come from the full verified catalog (party games listed first); every link is a real, verified game.
