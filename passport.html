Arcade passport

Every shelf, stamped.

The cabinet keeps a passport. Browse the Collections page and each shelf that scrolls into view earns an ink stamp here; finish every step of a guide's checklist and the guide leaves its mark. Counts come only from what this browser actually saw and ticked, nothing is estimated, and nothing leaves the browser.

    Guide marks

    Visa pages

    A mark is earned when every step of a guide's hands-on checklist is ticked, the checklist itself is the record.

      How a stamp lands. Open Collections and scroll: the moment a shelf card is on your screen, it is stamped with that day's date. Coming back does not re-stamp or re-date it, the first visit is the one in the book.

      What is not counted. Playing games happens on other sites, so the passport never claims you played or finished a game. For that record, see the Trophy Room, which tracks the marks you make yourself.
