Genre deep dive · 15 games

Multiplayer games: bring everyone.

Multiplayer is the cabinet's crowded room: 31 of the catalog's 213 games. This dive curates fifteen of them: the drawing rooms, the word games and the liars, the board-game table, the .io arenas, and the couch chaos. Every game below is free in the browser.

» Start here: draw & guess together

✏️

skribbl.io

Why it fits: online Pictionary with strangers, or a private room for just your friends, the record's own fork in the road, and hilarious either way, it says. Start here; every drawing room since is measured against it.

Controls: Mouse (draw)

☎️

Gartic Phone

Why it fits: telephone meets drawing, a sentence becomes a sketch becomes a guess, and the record promises the results are always unhinged.

Controls: Mouse (draw)

🦕

Drawasaurus

Why it fits: draw and guess with rooms for friends: Pictionary night, online, in the record's own four words.

Controls: Mouse (draw)

» Word games & liars

🕵️

Codenames

Why it fits: the official online version of the word-association party classic, one word of clue, a table of guesses, and a spymaster holding their nerve.

Controls: Mouse

💣

JKLM.fun

Why it fits: fast party word games: BombParty and PopSauce, named by the record itself, right in your browser, no lobby small talk required.

Controls: Keyboard

🎩

Secret Hitler

Why it fits: social deduction in 1930s Germany, says the record: lie convincingly or sniff out the fascists. The table will not trust each other afterwards.

Controls: Mouse

» The board-game table

🎲

Board Game Arena

Why it fits: hundreds of real board games, playable live with friends around the world, the record's count and the record's table, both enormous.

Controls: Mouse

♞

Lichess

Why it fits: completely free chess, matchmaking, puzzles, tournaments, and no account needed to play, says the record. The oldest duel on the shelf, with the shortest queue.

Controls: Mouse

🃏

PlayingCards.io

Why it fits: a virtual tabletop: play any card game with friends, your rules, the record hands you the deck and steps out of the way.

Controls: Mouse

» .io arenas: everyone for themselves

🦠

Agar.io

Why it fits: eat or be eaten in the cell arena that defined .io games, the record's verdict, and the whole family tree starts at this cell.

Controls: Mouse

🐍

Slither.io

Why it fits: the multiplayer snake: coil around rivals and feast on their remains. The record's menu is short because the loop needs nothing else.

Controls: Mouse

🐭

mope.io

Why it fits: eat, evolve, survive, climb the food chain from mouse to dragon in this multiplayer survival arena, exactly as the record lays out the climb.

Controls: Mouse + W

» Couch chaos & big lobbies

⚽

HaxBall

Why it fits: top-down physics soccer with friends, simple, perfect, timeless, says the record, and three adjectives is all a physics pitch needs.

Controls: Arrows + X

📱

AirConsole

Why it fits: turn every phone into a controller: 100+ party games on one big screen, couch co-op with zero installs. The record's pitch is the whole setup guide.

Controls: Phones as controllers

🏃

Stumble Guys

Why it fits: 32-player obstacle-course chaos in the official web version, the record counts the lobby, and the lobby is the game.

Controls: WASD + Space

What makes a game multiplayer? Another human on the other side of the move. These fifteen each seat the room differently, the drawing room (skribbl.io, Gartic Phone, Drawasaurus), the word table and the liar's circle (Codenames, JKLM.fun, Secret Hitler), the board-game table (Board Game Arena, Lichess, PlayingCards.io), the open arena where everyone is food (Agar.io, Slither.io, mope.io), and the couch at full volume (HaxBall, AirConsole, Stumble Guys). The other 16 multiplayer games in the catalog, a carrot brawler and a monster-battle simulator, more social deduction and a settlers-style trader, meme captioning, voxel sandboxes and base-builders, battle royales on land and sea, an ocean food chain, word battles, a tank MMO, and card-table classics, are all on the directory shelf.
