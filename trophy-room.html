The Trophy Room.

Every game you mark played or beaten lands here — per-genre completion dials, a 16-prize achievement case, and collection progress. It all lives in your browser, never on a server. Mark games with the Played / Beaten tokens on any game card.

» Backup & restore

Export

Download your whole trophy wall as one JSON file — played, beaten, favorites, streaks, prizes. Keep it somewhere safe; it is the only copy outside this browser.

Import

Paste a trophy export below (or pick the file) to restore it. The import is validated first and applied all at once — if anything is wrong, your current wall is left untouched.

Schema: bm.trophy.export · version

Reset

Wipe the whole wall — played, beaten, streaks, prizes — and start over. Your ♥ favorites are kept (they belong to the directory too). This cannot be undone (unless you exported first).

🛡️ Your data stays yours: trophies live in this browser's local storage under keys starting with bm.trophy. — favorites stay in bm_favs_v1 next to them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, ever. Clearing site data wipes the wall, so export if you care.
