Versus

Two games. Side by side.

Pick any two games in the catalog and Versus lays their real records next to each other, the year each description names, the shelves each game sits on, genre, controls, tags, the Kids corner audit, the writing that covers it. The verdict strip underneath reports only what those records support. No scores. No rankings. No invented winner.

• Versus • Records, Not Ratings •

Every line below is copied or counted from the catalog. If the records are thin, the strip says so.

1. Call the pairing

Any two of the 213 catalog games. The pairing lives in the page address (versus.html?a=…&b=…), so the link you copy is the face-off itself. Years come from the games' own catalog descriptions, every year, maker, and superlative claim in those descriptions was checked against an outside source by the Curator census; a description that names no year gets no year here.

The surprise deal is seeded, not random: an FNV-1a hash of your seed plus the sorted catalog ids feeds a mulberry32 generator, so the same seed against the same catalog deals the same pairing every time. The seed rides in the pairing link and on the share card.

Starter pairing on the marquee: the catalog's first game against its oldest dated record. Change either corner and it is your card.
