Genre deep dive · 6 games

Roguelikes: die, learn, die better.

Procedural runs, permanent death, and the good kind of "one more try". Every game below is free in the browser, no installs, no accounts, just you versus the dungeon.

» Start here

🐉

Dragonsweeper

Why it fits: the friendliest on-ramp in the genre. You already know Minesweeper's rules; now every number is a tactical decision about which monster to fight and which to sneak past. Runs last minutes, lessons last forever.

Controls: Mouse · Free on itch.io

🚪

Breach Command

Why it fits: pure tactical roguelike thinking with none of the reflex tax. Breach rooms, outthink the enemy, die, learn the layout logic, repeat. The cleanest "every death teaches you something" loop here.

Controls: Mouse / keyboard · Free

🗡️

Poom

Why it fits: a Doom demake squeezed into PICO-8's 128×128 pixels, procedural demon-blasting with permadeath stakes. Proof that the genre's tension survives any amount of graphical downgrade.

Controls: Arrows / WASD + Z X · Free on itch.io

» The full shelf

Hordes.io

Why it fits: the MMO-shaped roguelike: 3D dungeons with real permadeath and party play. Death costs you the character, which makes every dungeon pull genuinely tense.

Controls: WASD + mouse · Multiplayer

Celeste Classic

Why it fits: roguelike-adjacent by spirit: brutally precise climbing where every screen is a run and every death is instant, instructive feedback. The original PICO-8 build, 30 levels, zero mercy.

Controls: Arrows + Z X · Free on itch.io

Kaaarot

Why it fits: the genre's chaos dial turned to eleven, a 2-4 player party brawler about bouncing carrots. Not a traditional roguelike, but every round is a fresh procedural-ish scramble where nobody survives their own plan.

Controls: Keyboard (multi) · Free on itch.io · Great for game night

What makes a roguelike? The Berlin Interpretation checklist: randomized levels, permadeath, turn-based-ish or run-based play, and complexity that emerges from simple systems. These six each nail a different slice of it, tactical (Breach Command), puzzly (Dragonsweeper), action (Poom), social (Hordes.io), masochistic (Celeste Classic), chaotic (Kaaarot).
