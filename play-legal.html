The one clear page

Play it legal.

Emulation has a piracy problem. This site is the counterexample. Here's exactly where the line is — and everything on the fun side of it.

✅

Always fine

Emulators as software. EmulatorJS, RetroArch, mGBA — all legal, all open-source.

Your own dumps. Cartridges and discs you physically own, ripped with your own hardware.

Homebrew & public domain. Free ROMs by hobbyists, explicitly shared.

Legal archives. The Internet Archive's playable software collections.

⚠️

Your call, your risk

Abandonware. "Nobody sells it anymore" is not a legal doctrine — rights don't expire from neglect. We don't link it.

Fan translations & mods. Patches are usually fine; the base ROM usually isn't. Apply patches to your own dump.

BIOS files. Dump from your own hardware. Downloading someone else's is infringement.

🚫

Never here

ROM download sites. No links, no names, no hints. Ever.

Cracked / pirated games. No SteamUnlock-style sites, no "free download" traps.

Commercial ROMs of any kind. If you didn't dump it or buy it digitally, it doesn't belong in the Player.

BIOS downloads. Same rule as ROMs.

» FAQ

Short answers

  1. Is downloading a ROM of a game I own legal? — In most jurisdictions, no: owning the cart doesn't grant a download right. Dump it yourself instead.
  2. Is the Player itself legal? — Yes. It's a user-supplied-file tool built on open-source emulator cores. What you load into it is your responsibility.
  3. Why no ROM links "just for convenience"? — Because convenience is how piracy sites survive. The line stays bright.
  4. Can I share my converted browser game? — The file is yours to share, but the ROM inside it carries the same rights as the original. Share conversions of homebrew or your own creations freely; don't distribute commercial games.
  5. What about streaming my gameplay? — Generally fine under fair use / platform policies, but that's between you and the rights holder.

Not legal advice — just the lane this site drives in. When in doubt, buy it, dump it yourself, or play homebrew.
