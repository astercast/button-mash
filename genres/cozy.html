Genre deep dive · 11 games

Cozy games: no fail states, only vibes.

No timers, no game-overs, no stress. Just digging holes, building towns, rescuing ghost cats and scrolling into the abyss. Every game below is free in the browser.

» Start here

🏘️

Townscaper

Why it fits: the platonic ideal of cozy — click and a seaside town assembles itself, prettier than anything you could plan. No goals, no fail states, nothing to lose. Ten minutes here resets a bad afternoon.

Controls: Mouse · Free in browser

🐈

Six Cats Under

Why it fits: a ghost rescuing cats in a point-and-click afterlife — the rare cozy game with an actual story that earns its ending. Gentle puzzles, zero stakes, maximum warmth.

Controls: Mouse · Free on itch.io

🌊

The Deep Sea

Why it fits: not quite a game, better than one — scroll from sunlight to the trench, meeting real creatures at their real depths. Meditative, factual, and weirdly emotional at 8,000 meters.

Controls: Scroll · Free

» The full shelf

Serenitrove

Why it fits: dig a hole, find treasures, upgrade your shovel, dig a deeper hole. The coziest hole you'll ever dig — pure low-stakes progression.

Controls: Mouse · Free on itch.io

goodbye, doggy

Why it fits: short, tender, free — help a family as their ghostly dog. Cozy doesn't always mean cheerful; sometimes it means held.

Controls: Mouse · Free on itch.io

A Tale of Crowns

Why it fits: a sprawling fantasy interactive-fiction romance — the book-club entry on this shelf. Read at your own pace; the crown can wait.

Controls: Mouse / keyboard · Free on itch.io

Slow Roads

Why it fits: endless procedural driving through generated landscapes. No destination, no traffic, no hurry — pure zen with a steering wheel.

Controls: Arrows / WASD · Free

Fallen London

Why it fits: the slow-burn cozy: a gothic narrative RPG beneath a stolen London, with years of free stories. Sip it like tea, one storylet at a time.

Controls: Mouse · Free

Sandspiel

Why it fits: a falling-sand toy — fire, water, plants, acid, no score. The digital equivalent of a zen garden you can set on fire.

Controls: Mouse / touch · Free

Chrome Music Lab: Song Maker

Why it fits: paint notes on a grid and hear them back instantly. Making a tiny tune for a friend is a top-tier cozy activity.

Controls: Mouse / touch · Free

Nested

Why it fits: a universe inside a universe inside a universe, from the Cookie Clicker creator. Zoom forever; feel small in the nicest way.

Controls: Mouse · Free

What makes a game cozy? Three ingredients: no fail state (you can't lose), self-directed pace (nothing rushes you), and warmth — visual, narrative, or mechanical. Townscaper has all three; Fallen London trades mechanical warmth for narrative; Sandspiel is pure pace. Mix and match to taste.
