How a game gets in here

  1. Four bars, all mandatory: no open chat with strangers, no unmoderated multiplayer chat or user-generated content, no gambling mechanics, no graphic or horror content.
  2. When in doubt, it stays out. 70 of our 114 games did not make the cut — including good games with lobby chat, unmoderated player names, or content we could not verify.
  3. "👀 Play together first" means our assessment could not be fully verified end-to-end. Treat it as a co-op first session, not a verdict.
  4. Every "🔇 No chat" badge means the game has no way for strangers to talk to your child.
