Convert & download

We base64-encode your ROM into a game template bundling the EmulatorJS mGBA core. Big files take a minute — the progress bar keeps you posted.

Play & share the file

Double-click the downloaded .html to play instantly. Host it on any static host (GitHub Pages, itch.io, your own site) and it boots straight into your game — no installer, no emulator setup for the player.

How it works, honestly: your exported game contains your ROM plus a player stub. The emulator engine (EmulatorJS) loads from its public CDN on first run, then it's cached by the browser. So the file needs internet the very first time — after that it plays from cache. Save states persist in the browser via local storage.

🛡️ Legal lane: only convert files you own — dumps of cartridges you physically own, homebrew, or public-domain releases. Don't convert (or share) ROMs of games you don't own. Read the rules · How to dump your own cartridges

▸ Engineering notes

Size limits, stated plainly

GBA ROMs run up to 32MB; base64 embedding adds ~33% overhead, so the biggest exports approach 43MB. We warn above 16MB and refuse above 32MB — for big files, Player mode (no embedding, streams from your disk) is the better experience.

No BIOS needed

The export uses the mGBA core, which ships its own BIOS replacement — your game boots with zero extra files. Save states and in-game saves persist in the player's browser via local storage.
