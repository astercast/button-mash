Tournament night

One bracket. One champion.

The games take the bracket this time. Pick a saved lineup or a collection, and its real games are seeded in order — first game, top seed. Pick a winner in every match, round by round, until one game holds the marquee. Every pick saves in this browser, so the night is exactly where you left it.

• Tournament Night • Winner Stays On •

No invented games. No invented results. The bracket is the record.

1. Seed the night

Entrants come only from the verified catalog: a collection's games in shelf order, or a saved lineup's games in dealt order. Top seeds take honest first-round byes when the count needs them — the bracket says so on the ticket.
