How to choose

  • Just want to play in a tab? Use this site's Player — zero install.
  • One system, best experience? Grab the standalone pick above.
  • Many systems, one interface? Set up RetroArch and collect cores.
  • Accuracy above all? Mesen (NES), SameBoy (GB), mGBA (GBA) — the cycle-accurate crowd.
