Idle games: it plays itself (almost).

Idle is the cabinet's slow burn: 15 of the catalog's 213 games, and this dive curates all fifteen of them: the clicker canon, the slow stories, the prestige layers, the swarms and skill grinds, and the idle games that still hand you the wheel. Every game below is free in the browser.

» Start here: the clicker canon

🍪

Cookie Clicker

Why it fits: The idle game that popularized idle games, the record's own claim, and the reason this shelf exists. Bake cookies; achieve absurdity, it says, in that order.

Controls: Mouse (click)

What makes a game idle? Progress that keeps its own pace, numbers grow, worlds unfold, and your job is the occasional decision, not the constant click. These fifteen are the whole shelf: every idle game in the catalog is on this page, the clicker canon (Cookie Clicker, AdVenture Capitalist, Candy Box 2), the slow stories (A Dark Room, Universal Paperclips, Kittens Game), the prestige stacks (Antimatter Dimensions, Synergism, Realm Grinder), the swarms and skill grinds (Swarm Sim, Melvor Idle, Trimps), and the ones that hand some of the driving back (Bitburner, Stick Ranger, Prerolled).
