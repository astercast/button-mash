PortPlay for DOS

The DOS room. Your disks, in a tab.

DOS games never needed a cartridge; they need their files and a DOS to stand on. Drop a ZIP or a folder of a DOS game you own, and this room wraps it into a .jsdos bundle right here, DOSBox config included, then boots it. The bundle is the portable copy: download it, keep it, replay it any time. Nothing uploads, ever.

    How this room works, honestly

    The bundle is the converter output

    For cartridges, PortPlay makes a web page. For DOS, the honest portable form is the .jsdos bundle: your game's files plus a small dosbox.conf that mounts the folder and runs the start program. It plays here and in any js-dos player, and it is built entirely on your machine.

    Windows games are a different door

    A game that needs real Windows (95, 98, XP and later) is not a DOS game, and this room will not pretend a DOSBox wrap makes it one. Some have excellent source ports that read your original files; the guide shows how to find yours.

    The legal line: bring games you own: your old floppies and CDs, installed folders from your own machine, or the many DOS games their makers now give away. This room wraps and plays your files; it provides none. How to get your disks onto this computer.
